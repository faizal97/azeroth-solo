The professions update: every craft now goes to 300, Fishing and Cooking arrive, distance counts in every fight, and Rumhook Bay opens in the southern Vinewild with the Bloodsand Brawl.

**Professions up to 300**
- **Expert** (skill up to 225) from level 30 and **Artisan** (up to 300) from level 45 at any profession trainer. Every craft reaches about 300 by level 60.
- New veins, herbs, leathers and cloth by zone level, from Iron and Silk Cloth up to Duskiron, Moonsilver and Duskweave.
- About 85 new recipes: better potions and elixirs, **flasks** (2 hours, and they stay when you die), armour kits, weapon stones, and bags up to the rare 14-slot Starweave Bag.
- **Level-60 crafted sets** for a fresh 60 or an alt: Moonforged (mail), Wildrunner (leather) and Starweave (cloth), each with its own look in the wardrobe.
- Rare recipes drop from bosses and rares of their level, Artisan ones from level-60 bosses and Trials.
- **At 300**, each skill gives a keepsake you wear on your back and a title ("the Master Smith", "the Angler", "the Chef"…).
- The Alliance now has profession trainers in its level 52–60 camps, and Marshal's Refuge and Coldcoin have both factions' trainers.

**Fishing and Cooking**
- Two secondary skills up to 300 that take no profession slot.
- **Fishing:** tap "Fish here" at water, wait for the dip and tap. Big and rare fish fight on the reel. Auto lands common fish for you.
- **Cooking:** stop the needle in the gold for a Perfect batch. Well Fed meals give a stat buff for 30 minutes.

**Distance in every fight**
- Every ability has a range; out of range its button greys and shows the range it needs. The target frame shows how far away it is.
- Casters freeze and step back, melee charge and chase, snares slow, roots stop, fears make the target run.
- Ranged monsters cast and throw from range, flyers climb out of melee reach, and in a group your casters and healers stand behind the tank.
- **Step Back** for every class, and **Sprint** for rogues.

**Rumhook Bay and the Bloodsand Brawl (levels 35–40)**
- Rumhook Bay is a neutral goblin port in the southern Vinewild, with its own quests and a main-story stop.
- **The Bloodsand Brawl:** every 3 hours for 20 minutes, eight fighters at your level, three rounds of one on one. The champion picks a blue from the chest once a day. Journey → Open to you now shows when it opens.
- Main-story quests have their own mark.

**Auction house**
- A higher price is a real risk now: each price button shows how likely it is to sell within a day.
- Posting costs a small deposit, back when the item sells.

**Groups and dungeons**
- Dungeon bonus gear (Flawless, Speed bonus, Daily Roulette) fits your class, and a reward that doesn't fit in your bags goes to your bank.
- Bots in groups use openers (Ambush, Cheap Shot, Charge), and your group stands with you between pulls.
- "Flawless (no wipes)" says what it means, and on a roll for an item you can't use, Greed is the main button.

**What's new, in the game**
- This card: the first time you open a new version, it shows what changed. The main menu shows your version; tap it to read the notes again.

**Balance**
- Consumables at 60 are a modest edge, and raids and Trials are tuned without them.
- The three world bosses are now about as hard as each other: a wipe or two on average.
- Rogues: Sinister Strike hits harder, more Stamina each level, and Cloak of Shadows at 44 (Cold Blood moves to 54).
- Corvin Blackwell in the Smugglers' Deep calls one Blackguard.

**Fixes**
- Cooldowns keep running between fights (long ones came back early before).
- Party and guild invites and tips wait for a calm moment instead of opening over a fight, a dungeon or a screen you're using.
- Undead: Edric Fane, a level-4 rare, has moved from Last Bell to Spinner's Hollow. In every zone, a rare more than 2 levels above you sits below the normal creatures in the Fight list.
- A finished battleground closes on its own after time away and no longer blocks invites.
- Joining a battleground from a chat request works.
- No more accidental cutscene skips, and leftover taps after a kill no longer pull the next enemy.
- The news after time away names the dungeons and raids near your level (no spoilers from further on), and a bot no longer says "the the".
