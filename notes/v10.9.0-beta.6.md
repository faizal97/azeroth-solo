Test build. Only players with Settings → Beta updates on get it.

**Fixes**
- Joining a battleground from a chat group request no longer breaks the game: it starts the battleground with its two teams.
- Playing the main page after the beta no longer shows "Something went wrong" on Hero → Abilities: a skill the page doesn't know yet is skipped (and kept in your save).
- The guild invitation waits for a calm moment instead of opening in the middle of a fight or a dungeon.
- Undead: Edric Fane, a level-4 rare, has moved from Last Bell to Spinner's Hollow, where his quest sends you. In every zone, a rare more than 2 levels above you now sits below the normal creatures in the Fight list (its level still shows in red).
- Dungeon bonus gear (Flawless, Speed bonus, Daily Roulette) now fits your class: your armour, a weapon you can use and your stats. A reward that doesn't fit in your bags goes to your bank, and chat tells you.
- The Alliance has profession trainers in its level 52–60 camps (Drummond's Vigil, Greyfrost Camp, Brightwater Landing), and Marshal's Refuge and Coldcoin have both factions' trainers.
- Corvin Blackwell calls one Blackguard at his level minus 2 (beta.5's two adds made the fight far too hard for level-17 groups).

**Balance**
- The Hollow Colossus's chain lash hits for less: the three world bosses now take about one wipe on average.
- Rogues learn Cloak of Shadows at 44 and Cold Blood at 54 (swapped), so they have a survival tool when other classes get their stuns at 44.

**Smaller things**
- The Bags button gets a dot when an upgrade lands in your bags.
- Recipe lists show what still raises your skill first; grey recipes fold away with their count.
- Tips wait until no screen or dialog is open, so they don't cover what you tap.
- A trainer says "Artisan, the highest rank" instead of "fully trained for now".
- A guild you decline doesn't whisper its pitch right after.
- Fishing and cooking have their sounds: the cast, the bite, the reel, the catch, the sizzle and the burnt fizzle.
