Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Friends and cloud save**
- When you turn on Friends with cloud save off, the game asks **"Also back up your characters?"**
- **Turn on both** turns on Friends and cloud save in one Google window, so Google asks only once. **Friends only** turns on just Friends, and the game won't ask again on this device.
- If you allow only part of it in Google's window, that part turns on and the rest stays off. Closing the window turns on nothing.
- You won't see the question on itch.io or if cloud save is already on.

**Fixes**
- The map no longer shows the word "null" in zones with no rare hunt up.
- The Trophies screen no longer names places your character hasn't reached in the story. A trophy you haven't taken from there says "A place beyond your level" and sits in its own "Beyond your level" row. Trophies you've taken keep their names.
