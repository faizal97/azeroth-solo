Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Effects codex**
- **Hero → Journey → Effects** lists all 12 item effects, grouped by role. Each shows when it fires and whether your account owns it. Owned means worn, in bags or in the bank, on any of your characters.
- Tap an effect to read its rule at your best item's numbers (or a level-60 drop's if you have none), what it costs, and every item that carries it: its level, where it comes from, and who owns it.
- A source your character hasn't reached in the story says "A place beyond your level".

**Bots with effects**
- From level 50, bots in groups, raids and world parties sometimes wear an effect item. At level 60 it's about 1 in 4 for a skilled bot, fewer for less skilled ones and at lower levels.
- It's a real item in place of one of their pieces, not extra gear, and it fires just like yours, with the same callouts and numbers. Bot groups get a little stronger.
- Tap a group member's frame to see "◆ effect" and open the item. Sometimes a bot links its item in party chat when a run starts.
- A bot never rolls Need on an effect it already wears.
