Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Item effects**
- Some drops now carry an **effect**: a twist that reacts to what you do in a fight, paid for with part of the item's stats. An effect item is a choice, not a straight upgrade, so bags mark it ◆ instead of ▲.
- 12 effects. Damage: Opening Cut, Kindled Edge, Chase the Next, Steady Fuse, Glass Heart. Healing: Echoing Mend, Lifeline, Wellspring. Tanking: Turning Guard, Spiteful Hide. Also Tithe of Battle (resources) and Stubborn Blood (survival). Each one is strong in some fights and weak in others.
- Every dungeon's final boss drops one, and so does every raid boss (Hard drops it upgrade steps higher) and every world boss.
- The item card has an Effect line with real numbers, and after a run it says what the effect did ("Last run: 1,240 damage, 6% of yours"). Tap an effect's buff to read it.
- Mentor Mark upgrades raise an effect along with the stats.
- **Trial finds:** a Trial beaten in time has a 1 in 5 chance to drop that dungeon's effect item as a level-60 blue, never one you already have. The Trial briefing shows the chance and the item.

**Par times**
- Every dungeon and raid has a new par time: a steady group beats it about 1 run in 4, so beating par means something everywhere. Trials keep their own par.
