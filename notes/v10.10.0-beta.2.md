Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Item effects**
- An effect item now always keeps at least 40% of its stats, so none of them looks like a broken drop. Opening Cut, Kindled Edge, Chase the Next and Steady Fuse are a little weaker to pay for it.
- Effect epics that couldn't be upgraded (Cinderhide Bracers, Houndrunner Boots, Sabatons of the Hollow Choir, Molten Heart Leggings) now take Mentor Mark upgrades like the others.
- **Trial finds fit you:** if your class can't use a dungeon's own find, or you already have it, a timed Trial can bring another effect item from this month's Trials that suits your role. The briefing names it. Once you have them all, a winning roll pays Mentor Marks.

**Pace and levelling**
- **Fast pace** chains the next pull less often and goes into a boss a little lower on health: faster, and riskier where the fight can punish you.
- Drinks restore their mana in 12 seconds instead of 18.
- Bots soloing monsters now heal themselves when hurt.

**Fixes**
- New Hunters no longer start with a Warlock's imp; you tame your first beast at level 10. A Hunter who already has the imp keeps it until then.
- Section headings no longer run into a long subtitle (the Recipes list in Professions).
