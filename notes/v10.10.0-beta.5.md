Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Rare hunts (level 60)**
- Each level-60 rare now appears once in every 6-hour window and stays up for 30 minutes. Kill it and it stays down for the rest of that window.
- When one shows up, someone posts a **Rare sighting** in General, and you can tap it to go there. The map marks the place with an orange ◆ and "Rare hunt up now".
- While a rare is away, the Fight list says "Not here now" and when it was last seen. Its card shows its level, health and how hard it hits.
- They're a real fight now. The four non-elite rares hit about twice as hard as before, and the four elite rares are made for a full world party of three.

**Trophies**
- The first time any of your characters kills a level-60 rare or a world boss, your account takes its trophy. There are 11 so far.
- See them in **Hero → Journey → Trophies**: one row per zone. A plaque you've taken shows the creature, who took it and when. One you haven't taken shows its silhouette.
- Take 10 to earn the title **"the Big-Game Hunter"**.
- A rare's card says whether you have its trophy. With cloud save, trophies from both devices combine.

**Stronger bot groups**
- Bots in groups and world parties now carry weapons and capes made for their own level. Before, some carried a level-20 weapon long after level 20, so bot groups hit harder now.

**Balance**
- Four dungeons and raids have a slightly tighter par time, so at Normal pace a group still beats par about 1 run in 4. They include the Smugglers' Deep and the Tidehollow Deeps.
- Steady Fuse grows a little more slowly with upgrades.

**Fixes**
- In a dungeon, the pace rows under Tactics use the full width of a phone screen again.
- The Battle for Highmoor has its own picture in the group finder (crossed banners) instead of a dungeon boss's face.
