Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Fixes**
- The news after time away follows your level now. It names a dungeon only if it starts within 10 levels of yours, and raids and level-60 places only once you are 60. Clears further ahead are one line: "Veteran groups cleared 9 more places beyond your level." Server firsts you can't be told about yet are still kept for when you get there, and news an earlier build stored is tidied the same way.
- A long absence keeps its newest news, so the summary lines at the end are no longer cut off.
- "What's new" never shows to a brand-new player, on the first visit or any later one.
- The "What's new" card has an × at the top and a Got it button that stays at the bottom while you scroll the notes.
