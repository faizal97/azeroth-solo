Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Healer effects**
- **Lavish Mend** replaces Lifeline: your heals are 10% stronger and cost 25% more mana. It's on the same items, the Twinned Tide Bracers and the Sabatons of the Hollow Choir.
- **Tethered Mend** replaces Wellspring: each direct heal on the same ally in a row heals 5% more, up to 15%. A heal on a different ally heals 30% less and starts the count over. It's on the same items, the Bracers of the Quiet Page (and its Trial find) and Archivist's Tidering.
- **If you own one of these items, you keep it.** It now has the new effect, and an upgraded one keeps its upgrades. Neither new effect costs any of the item's stats.
- Upgrades grow Lavish Mend's extra healing and Tethered Mend's bonus. The extra mana cost and the penalty for switching allies stay the same.
- **Echoing Mend** costs a little more of the item's stats.

**Effect strength**
- Item cards show **Effect strength** under the Effect line: 100% as dropped, and higher with each upgrade.
- The upgrade dialog shows it before and after (for example "Effect strength 101% → 101.9%"), so you can see an effect grow even when its other numbers round to the same value.

**Fixes**
- Long messages wrap inside a phone screen instead of running off both edges, and the longer ones stay up a little longer so you can read them.
