Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Auction house**
- A higher price is now a real risk. At or below 0.8× the usual price an auction is certain to sell within a day; above that it gets less likely, about 8 in 10 at the usual price, 5 in 10 at 1.3× and 3 in 10 at 1.6×.
- Posting costs a small **deposit** (5% of the usual price). It comes back when the item sells and is lost if it doesn't.
- Each price button shows its odds and the deposit, so you pick knowing what you get: a fast sale, or a better price if you're willing to wait and repost.

**Professions**
- Every craft now reaches about 300 by level 60 without a grind after it. Blacksmithing and Tailoring were behind: Duskiron veins give more ore, several Artisan pieces are cheaper, new Duskiron Bracers (270), Silk Slippers (205) and Silk Bracers (210) fill the gaps, a Bolt of Silk takes 2 cloth, and humanoids from level 36 drop cloth two at a time. Each craft's first Artisan recipe can be learned from 200.

**Groups and dungeons**
- Bots in groups use openers now (Ambush, Garrote, Cheap Shot, Charge). A damage-dealer waits until the tank has hit the target.
- Your group stands with you between pulls, not only in fights.
- "Flawless" always says what it means: "Flawless (no wipes)".
- On a loot roll for an item you can't use, Greed is the main button (Need is still allowed).
- The Roulette card reads "The one dungeon you can reach" when only one is open.

**Fixes**
- A party invite no longer opens over a screen you're using: it waits for a calm moment, like the guild invitation and tips. If the other player has moved on by then, the invite lapses.
- A bank overfilled by rewards says so: "25 items in 24 slots: 1 over from rewards".
