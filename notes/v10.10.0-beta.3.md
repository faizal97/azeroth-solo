Test build. On Android, turn on Settings → Beta updates; in a browser, play it on the beta page.

**Priest and Druid level faster**
- **Word of Pain** (Priest) deals about 60% more damage.
- **Moonbeam** (Druid) burns for 12 sec instead of 9, about twice as much damage over time.
- Both show their new numbers in the ability text. Levelling solo as a Priest or a Druid is now closer to the other classes.

**Item effects**
- The **Hero** sheet lists the Effects you wear: each one's rule at your item's numbers, where it comes from, and "counts once" when two of your items share one.
- Dungeon briefings say how many of a boss's drops carry an Effect, and the boss card shows each one's Effect line.
- The loot roll shows an item's Effect line without opening it.
- A new effect item you can use lights the Bags dot, like an upgrade.
- In a fight, **Stubborn Blood**, **Steady Fuse** and **Lifeline** show a callout over your character when they fire, and every effect's damage and healing show in the effect colour.

**Effects and upgrades**
- The upgrade dialog shows an effect's numbers before and after, instead of "effects stay the same".
- Every effect now grows with Mentor Mark upgrades. Glass Heart's damage bonus grows (the extra damage you take doesn't), and Steady Fuse and Lifeline come round sooner: Steady Fuse every 42 sec instead of 45 at full upgrade.
- Effects grow more slowly than stats: 2–6% stronger at full upgrade (most about 3%). Before, most effects grew as fast as the item's stats, so a fully upgraded effect item shows smaller effect numbers than it did in the last beta.
- **Wellspring** refunds 90% of a critical heal's mana cost (was 80%). **Spiteful Hide** returns 1 less damage each time you're hit.

**Par times**
- 12 more dungeons and raids have a new par time, so at Normal pace a group beats par (and wins the speed chest) about 1 run in 4 everywhere. Trials keep their own par.

**Clearer screens**
- Tips about a fight or a run now show during it, as a banner your taps pass through. It closes with its × or after 7 sec.
- The pace choice shows Careful, Normal and Fast side by side: how long each rests, the health and mana it pulls at, and whether it pulls the next group.
- Under the tame buttons, a line says that taming another beast replaces your pet.
- On itch.io, the cloud save note now just says cloud save isn't available there, and that save codes move your characters.
