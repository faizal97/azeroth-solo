# The Ruinfall Saga: decisions

The main story after the Drowned Crown, as Faizal decides it. He drives the main saga himself (with the Lead); the
game designer records his decisions here, and places the small stories (Widya's Songbook, other Legend content)
around it. Started 2026-10-03.

**Status:** decisions only. Nothing is designed or filed yet, and nothing here is canon until it moves into
`docs/lore/canon.md` (with its Reveals and Names) when an expansion is designed. The three directions this grew from are
in `2026-10-03-v11-story-options.md`.

## Decided (Faizal, 2026-10-03, relayed by the Lead)

1. **The Ruinfall Saga: three expansions, one saga, ending with Ossarak, the Black Ruin.** Every expansion is a
   numbered, named chapter; devlogs use the form **"The Ruinfall Saga, Chapter 1: <name>"**.
   - **Chapter 1, v11:** working name "The Heir of the Ledger" (direction A in the options doc).
   - **Chapter 2, v12:** name to be decided.
   - **Chapter 3, v13:** name to be decided.
2. **Raids.** Chapter 1: one raid. Chapter 2: one raid. Chapter 3: one raid **plus a mega raid: Ossarak.**
   - **The mega raid is 20 or 25 players, and long** (the exact size is decided later).
3. **Not always about money.** In his words: "please we dont need to always evolve around debt or finance". Chapter 1
   closes the Ledger's story; the saga beyond it is not about debt or money.
4. **The Accord and the Krugar stay enemies through the whole saga. No peace and no alliance.** In his words: "we need
   to keep Accord vs Krugar please. i dont want them to be on peace first". Each faction keeps its own way in (separate
   staging, as for Veshmira's Lair), and the war between them goes on through every chapter.
5. **One new region or continent for the whole saga, not one per chapter.** In his words: "can we only create a new
   region or continent once on this saga? i dont want to always create new region or continent every single
   expansion". The other chapters use and change the world that exists.

## Agreed as a direction (still open to his refinement)

6. **Chapter 1's ending reveals that the Ledger served Ossarak's return:** Veshmira and Kethriax were working toward it.
7. **Chapter 2 can tie the ancient Drowning to Ossarak's first tearing of the world** (the Heartfire's bursting ten thousand
   years ago, and the Black Ruin's wound in the world).

## Still open (for Faizal)

- **Which chapter brings the one new region or continent** (his next decision).
- Chapter 2's and 3's names, places and spines.
- The mega raid's exact size (20 or 25), its length, and what gates it (Chapter 3's raid first?).
- How Ossarak relates to the Black Brood (canon doesn't say).
- One release or two per chapter (story and dungeons, then the raid), as the Drowned Crown did.

## Facts for when it's designed (not decisions)

- **Raids today hold 10 players** (2 tanks, 3 healers, 5 damage dealers). A 20 or 25-player raid is new for the engine,
  the raid sims and the phone screen: 25 party frames have to fit and stay readable (design mindset §2). It needs its
  own design pass before Chapter 3 is built.
- **Separate staging per faction** in every chapter means each one needs two ways in (two quest hubs, or two landings),
  as the Drowned Crown had.
- **Chapters that change the existing world** (rather than add a region) are mostly zone changes, quests and story
  scenes in places players know. Saves must keep working when a zone changes (migrate, never break).

## The game designer's part (filler, placed around the saga)

- **Widya's Songbook** follows her own want, not the world's fate (canon). One chapter per expansion at most, tied to
  a place the saga visits.
- **Other Legend content:** Lyveus's thread (Silverleaf watched Ossarak's scar) fits the saga itself, so it's Faizal's
  call; Bromli's cameos and a fourth Legend (when a creator brings one) are filler.
- Filler never reveals or decides anything in the main saga, and goes through the lorekeeper like everything else.
