# Legends as story heroes: design

2026-09-30. Agreed with Faizal.

## Why

Legends were built to "fight at your side": after their questline they took a slot in every Group Finder run. Two
problems followed.

- **Too strong.** Measured over 10 runs each, a group with Lyveus had about 90% fewer wipes and ran about 40%
  faster (Cinderpeak Depths: 3.6 wipes and 13.3 minutes with bots only, 0.2 and 7.9 with him). Most of it is his
  gear: always a full blue set, while most bot tanks are worse off. With Widya (a healer at 40) next to him, most
  dungeons would clear themselves.
- **Ordinary.** Always there, a Legend becomes the best tank in the queue, silent and taken for granted.

Real Friends (v10.1.1) now covers "people on the realm with you". So Legends take a different place: **they are the
heroes of the story.** Hand-made characters, most of them created by the developer's friends, whom the world and the
Black Ledger turn around.

## What a Legend is

- **Story.** Their questline, and a real place in the main story (Lyveus frees Hale at 60; Widya's ballad is how the
  realm learns Hale is alive at 40), with cutscenes, Lore Journal pages and the Theater.
- **Fights.** Always beside you in their own story fights and dungeons. Before you know them, a rare "stranger
  steps in" moment (as Lyveus's hooded knight does now). After their questline, **a rare cameo**. Never a standard
  group slot.
- **In the world** after their questline: found in a hub, later epilogue quests, appearances at story moments.
  (Writing work per Legend; done with Widya's story, then for Lyveus.)
- **Reward for finishing:** a title and a keepsake with a look, not combat power.

## The cameo

- Once a Legend's questline is done, each Group Finder run (dungeon, raid, Wanted) has a **1 in 5 chance** of that
  Legend joining, **at most once every 3 days** (real time) per Legend, and **one Legend per run**.
- They take their role's slot (a damage slot if you play that role), fight at the group's level with their own
  abilities, and wear **green gear** (not blue), so a cameo run is easier but not a walkover.
- **The entrance, every time, without stopping the game:** a name banner across the scene ("Lyveus Cloveus joins
  your group"), a short sound, their name and party lines in the Legend colour (orange), and a greeting.
- **The first cameo only:** a short scene of about 5 seconds as they walk in, one line, skippable, never repeated.
- **The goodbye:** a party line when the run ends.
- **The memory:** the Hero screen shows "Fought beside you 4 times · last in Graymouth" under the Legend.
- The Hero screen switch becomes **Cameos on / off**.

## Rewards

- A title per Legend, unlocked by the last quest of their story. Lyveus already has one ("%s the Oathkeeper"), and
  titles unlock from a finished quest, so nothing changes for players who finished him.
- A keepsake with a look (Lyveus: his leaf-and-pearl shield). New art, drawn as its own step.

## Steps

1. The cameo (no standard slot, 1 in 5, 3 days, one per run, green gear), its greeting, goodbye, banner, sound and
   colour, the memory line, the Cameos switch. Sim: cameos stay rare, the story fight still has the
   Legend, a switched-off Legend never comes.
2. The first-cameo scene.
3. The keepsake art.
4. A composed entrance sting (music ships only after Faizal listens; the first step reuses the quest-done sound).
5. Widya: her story (her friend is choosing between the three ideas), the Bard class kit, her questline at 20–40.

Parked: the roadmap still uses the old Warcraft names in its Legends and story sections.
