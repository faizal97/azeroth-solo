# v10 art audit

Status: audit done 2026-09-30, redraws not started. All art is our own SVG, so nothing is copied; the question is
which designs a Warcraft player would still recognise. Sheets: `node art/story/render.js` (story) and
`node art/render.js` (mobs, races, scenes, icons).

## A. Recognisably Warcraft: redraw

| Art | Why it reads as Warcraft | New look |
|---|---|---|
| `story:vancleef_story`, mob `vancleef` (Corvin Blackwell) | VanCleef's red bandana mask, tricorn hat and red cape | A tired shipwright: grey hood, leather apron over a coat, a shipwright's adze and a rolled ledger, no mask |
| `story:defias_crowd`, Defias mobs (Grey Hood thug, bandit, digger…) | The Defias red masks and hoods | Grey hoods and grey scarves, collectors' satchels; the name finally matches the look |
| Murlocs (Mireling streamrunner, forager, coastrunner…) and the chef (Crumbs) | Warcraft's murloc: fish-man with a fin crest and a huge mouth; the chef hat is Cookie | Marsh newts: smooth skin, frilled neck, webbed hands, reed spears; Crumbs keeps a ladle but loses the chef hat |
| Kobolds (worker, vermin, laborer, tunneler) | The candle on the head ("you no take candle") | A dented miner's helmet with a small lamp, or bare-headed with a lantern on a pole |
| `story:ragnaros` (Vulcarn) | Ragnaros: horned lava lord with a great hammer rising from a fire lake | A sleeping king of cooling basalt, crowned with glowing spikes, eyes half open, no hammer; wakes cracked and burning |
| `story:lady_prestor` (Lady Thorne) | Katrana Prestor's black hair and red gown | The Mistress of Coin: auburn hair pinned up, a dark green gown, a ledger and a quill, a chain of office |
| `story:prestor_shadow` | A winged dragon-woman: now also wrong, since Thorne is human | A tall cloaked figure at a desk of coins in the dark, one clawed shadow on the wall behind it (the hint, not the reveal) |
| Night elf race (Wood Elf) | The purple skin and glowing eyes | Natural skin tones (fair to olive and brown), green and gold clothing, hair in natural colours |
| Troll race and trolls (Kessari, Grimtooth…) | Warcraft's troll: blue or teal skin, huge tusks, mohawk, two-toed feet | Tall and lean with grey-green or sand skin, small tusks, braided hair, shell and bone jewellery, ordinary feet |
| Nightsaber (Shadowcat) | The purple-striped panther | A smoky grey cat with dark rosettes and pale eyes |
| Hearthstone icon (Map button, queue) | The blue swirl stone | A waystone: grey stone with a carved rune |

## B. Warcraft-flavoured: adjust

| Art | Note | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Tauren race (Hornfolk) | Bull-folk with nose rings and braids read as tauren | Keep them big and horned; switch to curled ram horns and a longer face, drop the nose ring |
| Undead race (Undead) | The exposed jaw and ribs are the Forsaken look | Grey skin, stitched cloth, sunken eyes; keep the jaw whole |
| Furbolgs (Bearkin) | Tribal feathers and headbands | Plain bear-folk with bark and moss |
| Wendigo | White fur, blue face, curled horns | Keep the white fur; lose the horns, add frost in the fur |
| Goblin shredder (Snork's Shredder) | A walker with a saw arm | Make it a tracked logging cart with a saw on the front |
| Wood elf scenes (Greatbough) | The purple night forest is Teldrassil's palette | Warmer: green canopy and gold light at dusk |
