# v11 story: options

For Faizal to choose. Planned for November or December 2026. Drafted by the game designer from `docs/lore/canon.md`
(2026-10-03). Nothing here is canon until he picks; then the chosen direction gets a design and canon gets its new
Reveals and Names.

The level cap stays 60 (the roadmap: after 60, progression is horizontal), so v11 is level-60 story content like
the Drowned Crown: a chapter, new places, dungeons, a raid, Legends, and collections, not more levels.

## 1. Where the story stands: open threads

**Set up on purpose, never paid off (the main story's own loose ends):**
1. **Kethriax and the Spire.** Lord Kethran Vale is Veshmira's eldest, keeps the Ledger's vault in Cinderpeak Spire
   and "waits to inherit". Canon: "The Spire stays shut: Kethriax is set up for later content." Never fought.
2. **The deed.** At 60 Thorne hands over the deed: "the kingdom is already sold". Veshmira is dead, but nothing says
   the debt died with her. Who holds the Ledger's books now?
3. **Tamlin's coronation**, "the day the crown's whole debt falls due". It hasn't happened.
4. **Reedsong's debt.** Widya won the lute back, but "the debt is not paid: that is the main story's to settle".
5. **The rest of Marrow's cabal.** Five years ago "a circle of nobles" plotted to sell the kingdom's soldiers. Marrow
   is dead; the others are never named.
6. **Vyn**, Lyveus's friend, who faked his death and "now writes from Coppergulch". Never seen.
7. **Thorne in Kingsmere Gaol**, who "kept her word to the last" and knows the Ledger's books better than anyone.

**Older and bigger:**
8. **Ossarak, the Black Ruin**, who "tore the world open", was driven off and never destroyed. Silverleaf Lodge was
   founded to watch his old scar. Canon never says how he relates to Veshmira's Black Brood.
9. **The Krugar's own enemies:** the Hollow Eye (orc warlocks after the old demon pacts), the Hollow Host (the plague's risen dead, from Wexmoor), the Order of the Pyre, and
   Graymouth's Grand Crusader, who "is not what he seems" (never said more). The Krugar's story has run "alongside"
   the Ledger's, never at the centre.

**Smaller, good for side stories:** Harrowby (who once lost a village to a debt), the Saltpenny trading house in
Rumhook Bay (deep in the Ledger's debt), the Blackgull pirates who rob the Ledger's captains.

**Closed:** Veshmira (dead), Vulcarn (the Magma Throne), Aeldran and Nal'veshra (the Drowned Crown), Marrow (dead at
60), Hale (free), Widya's lute, Bromli's ballad.

The **Hollow Eye** isn't described in canon.md, but the Lore Journal defines it (`src/data/lore_places.js`): orc
warlocks who want back the old demon pacts the Krugar gave up, holding their rites in the caves of the Smoke Pit and the
Blooding Grounds (Varrok the Invoker). It belongs in canon.md's Names before any new story uses it.

## 2. Three directions

### A. "The Heir of the Ledger" (recommended)

**The spine.** Veshmira's death didn't cancel a single debt. Her eldest, Kethriax, opens the Spire and takes up the
Ledger's books, and he calls everything due on the one day canon has been pointing at all along: **Tamlin's
coronation**. He doesn't want to own the kingdom like his mother did; he wants it to *pay*, in gold, in land, in
soldiers.

**How each faction enters.**
- **Accord:** the coronation in Kingsmere. Carrow and Hale try to hold the day together; Thorne, in the Gaol, is the
  only one who can read Kethriax's books, and the player has to bargain with her.
- **Krugar:** "whatever banner you fly, the Ledger holds your debts". Kethriax calls in the war chest High Chief
  Grask borrowed against, and the Krugar strike back at the Ledger's southern counting-houses (the Saltpenny house
  in Rumhook Bay).
- Both meet at the Spire.

**What it adds (a sketch).** A level-60 zone around Cinderpeak Spire's upper reaches; a five-player dungeon per side
(the Accord's coronation fight in Kingsmere, the Krugar's raid on Saltpenny's counting-house); a shared 10-player raid
into the Spire's vault, ending with Kethriax in his true form.

**What it reveals and closes.** Threads 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7. The deed burns in the Spire, and with it Reedsong's
note. The cabal's other names are in the books. Vyn comes back. Thorne's last scene.
**What it opens.** Ossarak: Kethriax's last words say his mother wasn't the oldest of the Brood.

**Why I recommend it.** The Ledger is this game's spine. Its biggest set-ups (the shut Spire, the deed, the
coronation) are exactly what the game has been promising since the prologue, and leaving them hanging weakens
everything before them. It reuses places players already know (Kingsmere, Cinderpeak, Rumhook Bay), so most of the new
work goes into the story, the raid and one zone. And it ends by opening the bigger story (B) for v12.

### B. "The Black Ruin"

**The spine.** Ossarak returns to the scar he tore in the world, which Silverleaf's elves were founded to watch.
Lyveus is at its centre. A new zone around the scar, and a raid against the Black Ruin himself.
**For:** the biggest, most mythic story; Lyveus's home finally matters.
**Against:** it skips the Ledger's open set-ups (Kethriax, the deed, the coronation), so they'd hang behind a bigger
story. And it needs the most new art (a new region and a world-scale dragon).
**Better as v12,** set up by A.

### C. "The Pale Crusade" (the Krugar's turn)

**The spine.** The Krugar's own thread at the centre: Graymouth's Grand Crusader is not what he seems, the Order of the
Pyre and the Hollow Host are two halves of one plan, and the Hollow Eye's warlocks are bargaining with the same dark, and it leads north into the heart of fallen Wexmoor. The Accord
enters through the Lantern Watch at Greyfrost Camp.
**For:** it evens out the factions (the Krugar finally lead), and it uses threads canon has seeded since the start.
**Against:** it leaves the Ledger's ending hanging, and it's a different tone (undead and crusaders after a story
about debt).
**Could be** woven into A's Krugar side, or come later.

## 3. The Legends

- **Lyveus:** central in A (guarding the coronation; the cabal that burned his home is in Kethriax's books) and in B
  (his lodge watched the scar).
- **Widya:** her Songbook follows her want, not the world's fate (canon). In A that fits exactly: a Songbook chapter
  where Reedsong's note is found in the Spire's vault and burned, and she writes the song of the day the hamlet
  stopped owing.
- **Bromli:** never part of the Ledger story, and he never asks. In A he turns up because a coronation feast and a
  dragon fight are the best chance yet for a ballad. A comic cameo, not a questline.
- **A fourth Legend:** the roadmap's rule is "when a creator brings one". None is due. Vyn comes back as a story
  character, not a Legend.

## 4. What to decide first, and the size

**Faizal decides, in this order:**
1. **The direction:** A, B or C (or A with C's Krugar thread folded in).
2. **One release or two:** v11.0 with the story, the zone and the dungeons, then v11.1 with the raid (as the Drowned
   Crown did), or everything at once.
3. **Kethriax's goal** (for A): pay or own. Pay (my pitch) makes him different from his mother.
4. **Bromli and Widya in it:** yes or no (they're cameos and a chapter, not the spine).

**Then the game designer** writes the design (zone, dungeons, raid bosses, the story beat by beat) and canon's new
Reveals and Names, and files the issues.

**Size, from this team's measured throughput** (a beta has taken about 20 minutes to 6 hours): direction A is about
**8 to 10 betas**, roughly **35–50 hours of developer work**, plus about **6–10 hours of sim CPU** (dungeon and raid
tuning). The real limits aren't build time: they're **Faizal's reviews** (art contact sheets, cutscenes, and the music,
which ships only if his ears approve it) and his release pace. A November release is comfortably in reach if the
direction is chosen in October.
