Realm of Loner

A world of your own

A free single-player online RPG for Android and the browser. Everyone else on the realm is simulated: they level, chat, form groups, run dungeons, win and lose loot rolls, and join your party in the open world. You get the feel of a busy server in a game you can put down at any time.

Play in your browser Android app

The game

  • An original world, Caldreth, with its own story, The Black Ledger, told in cutscenes, quests and a Lore Journal.
  • Two factions, the Accord and the Krugar. Any race can play any class.
  • Hand-drawn scenes and sprites, real combat maths, gear, quests, dungeons and raids, and a Group Finder full of simulated players.
  • Working chat and guilds: simulated players ask for groups, trade, recruit and remember you.
  • The world keeps going while you are away.

Your saves

Characters are saved on your own device, and you need no account to play. If you like, you can sign in with Google in Settings → Cloud save to keep a copy of your characters in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, so you can carry on across your phone and the browser. The game only sees its own folder, and we never receive your saves. With the optional Friends feature (Social → Friends) you add real players by friend code and see each other's characters and gear; it runs on Google's Firebase and shares only what you choose, with the friends you accept. Read the privacy page for the details.
